all right children are greater than their parent. all left children are lower than their parent
(note ,  dont assume this condition only holds for the root, it would be poor reading comprehension to assume that )

an AVL tree is a BST plus one single additional invariant, applied to every node:
The balance condition

    For every node: |height(left subtree) − height(right subtree)| ≤ 1

where height is the number of nodes between the current node. and the furthest away leaf that is a child of the current node
